# Site packet: use these files locally

**Open on:** Soi Ramkhamhaeng 144, Saphan Sung, Bangkok. Camera target `[100.6930843, 13.7847500]` in **longitude, latitude** order. Start around zoom 17.5–18, pitch 48–55°, then tune on a laptop. At least one cross street and several rows of neighbors should remain visible. `example-plots.geojson` includes a second real street to make “change road” testable without borrowing a road that doesn't border the first plot.

| File | What's actually in it | What it does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|
| `example-plots.geojson` | Two constructed, closed WGS84 rings. First is a roughly 22 m × 22 m plot with a 5 m × 6 m notch. Using EPSG:32647 its area is **453.91 m²**, about 3.00 m from the mapped centerline. Second is a five-sided parcel at Soi 142/1. `front_edge` indexes the **original ring**; preserve endpoints/IDs if you rewind it. | Not cadastral geometry, ownership, proof of vacancy, public-road designation, or a ROW survey. Widths 6 and 8 m are **scenario inputs**. Do not say measured. |
| `roads.osm.geojson` | **94** real OSM `highway` ways with coordinates. See [way 157453167, Soi 144](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/157453167) and [way 157453542, Soi 142/1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/157453542). Some are access drives or paths; render by class. Ways may continue beyond the query box, so bound the camera. | A centerline is not a parcel boundary or a legal ROW width. Do not treat every `highway` or service way as a public frontage. |
| `buildings.overture.geojson` | **559 actual mapped polygon footprints**, Overture Maps buildings release **2026-11-18.0**. Per-feature source: Google Open Buildings 380, Microsoft ML Buildings 177, OSM 2. Stable feature ID, source and geometry retained. **0/559 supplied positive height or storey count.** | Not surveyed as-built geometry or verified heights. Use uniform illustrative massing (e.g. 6 m) with a persistent legend, “Mapped footprints · heights illustrative.” Don't invent precise towers or call it a photorealistic digital twin. |

**Why two sources:** an initial OSM box just south of this one had zero building-tagged ways. The final box had only two. OSM roads + Overture footprints resolve that gap and can ship as static assets. Do not download third-party raster tiles for offline reuse or make Overpass a live dependency.

### Zone evidence and scope window

Use the [published 2556 land-use map, sheet 8](https://download.asa.or.th/03media/04law/cpa/BMAlanduse/mr56-bma-08b.jpg) and [BMA's district map index](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/cpud/page/sub/22628). On sheet 8 locate the Ramkhamhaeng arterial running NE–SW, its cloverleaf with Kanchanaphisek, then the yellow block **southeast of the arterial and east of the interchange** labeled `ย.๓-๓๖`. The sois run southeast from the arterial. Do not use the school parcel, the arterial intersection or land north of it as the demo. The numbered subarea, not a yellow swatch by itself, is the useful evidence.

This is map-scale identification, **not** an authoritative digital zone polygon. For v1, use a small interior **study window**, e.g. `[100.69155,13.78350,100.69385,13.78520]` (west, south, east, north), covering the supplied parcels. Label it “supported study area,” never “official zone boundary.” Show the linked source map and chosen zone in the UI. For a new drawing here, show “Analyzing under ย.3; confirm planning designation.” Fail closed beyond the window, or allow *explicit* hypothetical mode without a verified-zone badge. If adding official vector zoning later, intersect the entire plot and check every component, including exclusions, rather than classifying a centroid.

The **larger context fetch box** below is for surroundings only. Do not offer compliance analysis everywhere in that box. The GIS endpoint was not accessible from this session; the app does not need to rely on it.

### Reproducibility and attribution

Retrieved 20 Nov 2026. Box `[100.690,13.7828,100.696,13.7885]`. The context is about a neighborhood block group, not a Bangkok download.

1. Roads: [OSM API extract for this box](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=100.690,13.7828,100.696,13.7885). Join way node IDs to node lon/lat in the given order. Keep only complete ways with a `highway` tag. Strip editor/user metadata; preserve ID, names, highway class, width/access/bridge/layer where available. The live OSM response may change; use the checked-in snapshot for tests.
2. Buildings: [Overture quickstart](https://docs.overturemaps.org/getting-data/), [buildings guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/buildings/). The retrieval used `overturemaps download --bbox=100.690,13.7828,100.696,13.7885 -f geojson --type=building -o buildings.geojson`. Confirm the downloaded release in its `.state` file; future default releases can differ. For this extract keep top-level ID, original polygon and dataset source; `height_m` and `levels` stay null where absent. No building is generated or placed at random.
3. OSM roads and Overture buildings: retain notices for [© OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [Overture Maps Foundation and building sources](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/#buildings), [Google Open Buildings](https://sites.research.google/gr/open-buildings/) and [Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints).
