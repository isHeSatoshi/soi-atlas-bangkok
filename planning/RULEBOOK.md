# Rulebook for the low-rise study

Research checked 20 November 2026. Use the Thai law, not this English interpretation, as the authority. This is a massing screen for one standalone building, not legal advice, a land entitlement or a building-permit assessment. Distinguish **law**, **interpretation**, **product limit** and **input assumption** in the rules drawer.

## Rules to encode

Let `A` be the projected plot area, `G` the sum of gross floorplate areas, `C` the area of the union of all roofed projections, `O=A−C` the uncovered ground. `W` is the **public legal right-of-way width**, not lane width or an OSM `width` tag. Use actual input measurements before rounding. All distances in m, all areas in m².

| Rule ID | Predicate and decision | How it affects the study |
|---|---|---|
| `Z-FAR` | `G ≤ 2.5 × A`. Z §10: `๒.๕ : ๑`. No bonus, no other buildings retained on the parcel, no prior subdivision allocations. | Gross floor budget. Show both legal budget and actual sum; do not confuse with footprint coverage. |
| `Z-OSR` | `O ≥ 0.125 × G`. Z §4 and §10: `ที่ว่างต่อพื้นที่อาคารรวม`, minimum **12.5% of GFA**. | Ground reservation or GFA reduction. At full base FAR this is **31.25% of land**, not 12.5% of land. |
| `B-OPEN`, `N-OPEN` | B §52(1/2): uncovered allowance modeled at **30% of land** for residential, **10% of land** for entirely nonresidential office. N §33: corresponding **30% or 10% of largest floor**. Model conservatively with open-to-sky ground only. | `requiredOpen = max(0.125G, pA, p × largestPlate)`. Biggest plate cannot exceed land in this model, so the national check is nonbinding. Take the maximum, not the sum. Separate the denominators in the UI. |
| `Z-PLANT` | Z §10: `พื้นที่น้ำซึมผ่านได้เพื่อปลูกต้นไม้ ... ร้อยละห้าสิบของพื้นที่ว่าง`. Reserve permeable planting for at least half the open space. Interpret conservatively as **half the actual modeled uncovered ground**. | Draw an allocated, non-roofed patch `P` inside `O`, excluding required access; compare `area(P) ≥ 0.5 × O`. Treat soil permeability as design intent, not a verified site measurement. |
| `Z-USE-RES` | Z §10(11): multi-unit residential **over 1,000 m²** is excluded except **over 1,000 through 10,000 m²** on a public ROW **≥30 m** or within the specified **500 m** transit-station condition. At 1,000 the “over” threshold has not been crossed. | Below 30 m, use 1,000 m² with no transit credit. Show the 10,000 m² conditional threshold for qualifying ROW separately; **still search only up to 1,000**. Do not infer station eligibility from a distance to a pin. |
| `Z-USE-OFF` | Z §10(13): ordinary standalone office defaults to **≤300 m² operating area**; over 300 through **500 m²** with ROW **≥16 m** or the specified station condition. Shophouse and research-office exceptions are distinct; not this program. | Count **all modeled GFA** conservatively as operating area. `W=15.99 → 300`, `W=16 → 500`. Do not relabel “office” as general retail. |
| `B/N-FRONT` | B §50 / N §41: for any building on `W<6`, at least **3 m from centerline**. For the enhanced class, `W<10`: **6 m from centerline**; `10≤W≤20`: **W/10 from near ROW**; `W>20`: **2 m from near ROW**. Enhanced applies to listed classes, or more than two storeys or >8 m. Bangkok has a small-residential exception; the national text does not state the identical exception. | With plot edge at the near ROW, convert centerline distances to `max(0, threshold − W/2)`. For multifamily use enhanced when `N>2 or H>8`; for office adopt enhanced at every height as a conservative classification. Take the stricter applicable branch; disclose conservative treatment, not exemption advice. |
| `B/N-SKY` | B §49 / N §44: from **any point** on the building to the **opposite** side of the nearest public road, height ≤ twice the perpendicular horizontal distance. Under flat site, straight ROW and near edge at `d=0`, **`z ≤ 2(W+d)`**. | At floor top `z`, clip to inward depth `d ≥ z/2 − W`. This is separate from the ground setback. No generic 2×road-width cap. No wide-road, intersection, receding-road or other exceptions claimed. |
| `B/N-SIDE` | N §50: walls with openings/terraces at least **2 m** from neighboring boundaries if total `H≤9`, **3 m** for `9<H<23`. B §54 adds **3 m** at floor 3 or above, or for parts higher than 9 m, against private land. | Assume openings on all private sides, no blank-wall/consent exception. For a tall candidate do not let the lower floors use the 2 m class. Apply the larger of total-height and floor-specific setbacks. A conservative 3 m at all private sides is OK if explicitly called a design inset; still test the statutory bands. |
| `B-RING` | B §55: all-round clear space **1 m** up to and including 15 m; **2 m** above 15 m. Small house exception not used. | Intersect all-round clearance with the other requirements; take max, not sum. The frontage may be controlled by this rather than the road-line rule. |
| `B-FRONTAGE` | B §§52(6), 53: claiming the roadside front-open-space exemption requires the **building façade**, not just the plot, to adjoin the road as defined: length **>1/8** of exterior perimeter and **≤20 m** from it. | Design only direct-frontage schemes: require a continuous, unobstructed front-facing edge aligned to the ROW on the ground footprint, check its length and distance. This is a conservative implementation of the frontage condition. If no qualifying façade, flag “interior-access case not modeled” rather than silently grant exemption; §52(6) has 6/12 m and access conditions. For office reserve an unobstructed ≥2 m rear walking strip as a conservative §52(7) treatment; exclude it from planting. |

**Enforcement details:** `frontNeededAt(z) = max(applicableRoadLine, allRound, z/2 − W)`. Remove private-edge strips independently. In the solver's section view show the actual threshold and the driver's name; ties are possible. To check a height plane on a polygonal horizontal roof, measure the minimum signed perpendicular depth over its vertices (an affine function reaches its minimum there). Also check full polygon differences for containment, not just vertices.

**Three distinct limits to report:** statutory base FAR, conditional use allowance, and what this **specific** low-rise design achieved. A neighborhood may admit more than the supported class. A home/office toggle need not change every constant: it changes use eligibility, open-ground minimum, program floor height and therefore designs. Show the meaningful differences.

## Inputs and exclusions

- The drawn outline represents assumed single ownership/redevelopment, not a survey. If it overlaps mapped buildings, state that the study assumes their removal; never erase neighboring structures or assume a partially intersected building is wholly on site. The supplied default does not need demolition in the footprint extract.
- Selected ROW, public status, flat road/site datum, openings, floor height and direct legal access are assumptions. Offer `assumed / user-entered` labels. Two-road corners, canals, nonparallel ROW, road reservations, easements and off-site access need a different geometry model. If a user draws a corner site, **accept the sketch** and explain why the one-frontage analysis is conditional or unsupported.
- Exclude high-rise/special/large-building design, subdivision/recombined FAR, transit/green FAR bonuses, underground floors, balconies/canopies projecting beyond the floorplates and rooftop equipment. No net saleable-area estimates. No permit claims for parking (B §83 has program/size triggers), fire egress, structure, sanitation, EIA, aviation, heritage, flood or utility overlays. Record unknowns as “not assessed,” not green checks.
- The BMA GIS service was not reliable from this session. Do not call it from the browser's calculation path. For zone edits use the map-backed small study window or explicit user-confirmed `ย.3 scenario`, not a citywide lookup based on a postal district, color alone or the parcel centroid.
